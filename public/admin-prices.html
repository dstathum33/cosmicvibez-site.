How the Studio price works. The customer drags the Engraving size slider and the price updates. Price = the item price for the size they pick + the size add-on for how big their engraving is (in square inches, width x height). Leave an item price blank and customers see "we'll reply with your quote" for it. A blank size add-on counts as $0. Prices are shown as "starting at" and are confirmed on your proof.

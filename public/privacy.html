01What we collect

When you send a request or buy something, we collect what you give us:

  • Your name and email address, and your phone number if you add one.
  • What you want made: your text, product choices, quantity, dates, budget and notes.
  • Files you upload, such as logos, photos and artwork, plus the preview image our Mock-Up Studio makes.
  • A delivery ZIP code, if you ask for delivery.

Your card details are entered on Stripe's secure checkout page. We never see or store your card number.

02How we use it

We use your information to quote, make and deliver your order, send you confirmation emails, proofs and payment links, answer your questions, and keep records of our orders. We never sell your information. We won't post photos of your custom piece without asking you first.

03Who else handles it

We use a few services to run the site and the shop. They handle your information only to do their part:

  • Stripe takes your payment.
  • Render hosts our website and stores your requests and uploaded files.
  • An email service sends our confirmations and alerts.
  • Google Fonts loads the fonts on our pages, so Google may see your IP address when a page loads.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested. We use your IP address to limit spam and repeated form submissions.

04Cookies and tracking

We don't run advertising or analytics trackers on this site. The only cookie we set is a sign-in cookie for the site owner's admin page.

05How long we keep it

We keep your request, files and order record as long as we need them to finish your order, handle returns or remakes, and keep our business records. After that we delete them.

06Your choices

You can ask us what we have about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Call, text or email us and we'll take care of it. Our site is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

07Changes

If we change this page, we'll update the "last updated" date above.

08Questions?

Ask before you order. We'd rather answer it now than fix it later.

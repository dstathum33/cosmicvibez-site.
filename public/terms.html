01Who we are

These terms are an agreement between you and CosmicVibezCreations LLC, doing business as Cosmic Vibez Creations, in Keyport, New Jersey ("we", "us"). By sending a request, placing an order or paying us, you agree to these terms and to our Store Policies. If something here doesn't work for you, please ask us before you order.

02How ordering works

There are two ways to order.

  • Buy now. For standard items and sizes with a listed price, you can pay the full price at checkout on our Mock-Up Studio. Buy now is for orders picked up in Keyport. Rush orders, delivery or shipping, items you bring us, sizes that aren't listed and larger jobs use a quote instead.
  • Get a Free Quote. Tell us what you want and we reply with an exact price and a payment link. Quotes are free and come with no obligation. Business and bulk orders may have their own payment terms, written into the quote.

Prices are in US dollars. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay for the items in your order.

03Payment

Buy now is paid in full at checkout. Quote orders are paid through the payment link we send you. Payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.

04Proofs and approval

After you order, we send you a digital proof showing the layout, wording and placement. Nothing is engraved or printed until you approve the proof in writing (text or email is fine).

  • Please check every name, date and spelling. We produce exactly what you approve.
  • A design is final once you and our designer reach a final agreement, on that same day. Changes after that aren't included.
  • Mistakes that were in an approved proof aren't covered for a free remake.

05Cancellations and refunds

  • Within 12 hours of placing your order: you can cancel for a full refund minus a design fee.
  • After production starts: the order can't be cancelled or refunded, because materials have already been used on your custom piece.

06Returns and remakes

Every piece is made to order and personalized for you, so we can't take it back for a change of mind. If we made a mistake, meaning the piece doesn't match your approved proof, or it arrives defective, we'll remake it or refund you. Contact us within 3 days of receiving it, with photos.

Wood grain, leather texture and stone are natural, and 3D prints can show fine layer lines. Color and pattern vary from piece to piece and from screen photos. Those natural differences aren't defects.

07Items you bring us

We'll tell you whether your item works with a laser before we start, and acceptance of customer items may vary. Every item reacts a little differently, and hidden coatings or flaws can't always be seen ahead of time. There is an up charge for bringing your own item, to cover any damage that may occur.

  • We own the designs we create. You can lease or purchase the copyright to a design.
  • We won't post photos of your custom piece without asking you first.

09Pickup and delivery

We're based in Keyport, New Jersey, and pickup is by appointment. If you need delivery or shipping, we'll confirm how it works and any cost in your quote before you pay.

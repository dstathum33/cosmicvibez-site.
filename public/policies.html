01Quotes

Quotes are free and come with no obligation. Pricing depends on the size, material and complexity of each piece, and on quantity.

A quote is good for Client to confirm: number of days. After that, material costs may have changed, so we'll re-quote.

02Proofs & Approval

Before anything is engraved or printed, we send you a digital proof showing the layout, wording and placement.

  • Please check every name, date and spelling. We produce exactly what you approve.
  • Designs are final once you and our designer reach a final agreement, on that same day. Changes after that aren't included.
  • Nothing goes into production until the proof is approved in writing (text or email is fine).

Mistakes that were in an approved proof aren't covered for a free remake. That's why we ask you to check it twice.

03Payment

We accept Client to confirm: payment methods.

Orders start after Client to confirm: full payment or deposit amount is received. Business and bulk orders may have their own payment terms, written into the quote.

04Turnaround

The clock starts once your proof is approved and payment is received, not when you first reach out.

  • Standard orders: Client to confirm: typical time and order size it applies to.
  • Bulk and business orders: timeline given in your quote, based on quantity.
  • Rush orders: Client to confirm: offered or not, and any fee.

Have a deadline, like a wedding or event date? Put it in your quote request so we can tell you up front if we can make it.

05Pickup & Shipping

We're based in Keyport, New Jersey. Client to confirm: local pickup, how it's arranged, and any local delivery.

Shipping: Client to confirm: offered or not, carriers, how cost is charged. We'll send tracking once your order ships.

If a package arrives damaged, send us photos of the box and the item within Client to confirm: number of days of delivery and we'll work it out with you.

06Returns & Refunds

Every piece is made to order and personalized for you, so it can't be resold. Because of that, custom items can't be returned or exchanged for a change of mind.

If we made a mistake, meaning the piece doesn't match your approved proof, or it arrives defective, we'll remake it or refund you. Contact us within 3 days of receiving it, with photos.

07Cancellations

  • Within 12 hours of placing your order: cancel for a full refund minus a design fee.
  • After production starts: the order can't be cancelled or refunded, since materials have already been used on your custom piece.

08Natural Materials & Finishes

Wood grain, leather texture and stone are natural, so color and pattern vary from piece to piece and from photos. Engraving depth and darkness can look different on each material. 3D prints may show fine layer lines, which are normal for the process. Colors on your screen may not match the finished piece exactly.

These natural variations aren't defects and aren't grounds for a return.

09Items You Bring Us

Want something of your own engraved? We'll tell you whether the material works before we start. Acceptance of customer items may vary by item.

Every item reacts to a laser a little differently, and hidden coatings or flaws can't always be seen ahead of time. There is an up charge for bringing your own item, to cover any damage that may occur.

10Artwork, Logos & 3D Files

  • By sending us a logo, photo or design, you confirm you have the right to use it. We can't reproduce trademarked or copyrighted artwork (team logos, characters, brand names) without permission from the owner.
  • For 3D printing, we review your file before quoting. If it needs fixes to print well, we'll tell you first. Charges may vary for file repair or design fees.
  • We own the designs we create. You can lease or purchase the copyright to a design.

11Privacy

We use your name, contact details and files only to quote, make and deliver your order. We never sell or share your information. We won't post photos of your custom piece without asking you first.

12Questions?

Ask before you order. We'd rather answer it now than fix it later.

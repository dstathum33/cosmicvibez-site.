Three ways we can help

3D printing

Send an STL, OBJ or 3MF file and we print it. Parts, prototypes, display pieces and gifts. Tell us the size, color and use, and we recommend the material.

3D modeling

No file yet? Send a sketch, photos or measurements and we design a model you can approve before we print it.

Need a copy of an existing part or object? We scan it into a digital model so it can be printed or changed.

Questions

What do I send for 3D printing?

A 3D file (STL, OBJ or 3MF) is best. If you only have a sketch, photos or measurements, choose 3D modeling and we design it for you.

Can you copy an existing part?

Yes, that is what 3D scanning is for. Tell us what the object is and its approximate size in your request.

How is 3D work priced?

Pricing depends on size, material, detail and how much design work is needed. 3D scanning to a mesh file has a base fee of $100 to $250, depending on complexity. CAD modeling, which turns a scan into an editable model, is $65 to over $200 per hour, depending on the work. Printing is priced by size, material and detail. Send a request and we reply with a free, no-obligation quote.

Do you also do laser engraving?

Yes. Cosmic Vibez Creations is best known for custom laser engraving. See the Mock-Up Studio to preview an engraving.
